Ship nodes show a tooltip on hover and keyboard focus: render with tech badge, name, element glyphs, an optional estimated price and the ship's bonuses per skill, role and misc, with units and bold item names. `ShipTree` and `ShipTree.Root` accept `prices` and `locale`. `TreeDisplay`'s `shipTooltip` turns it off or replaces its content. `FloatingTooltip` gains a `vertical` placement with up and down pointers.
